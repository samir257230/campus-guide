Authentic Metrics Beyond The Brochures

We collect 5 crucial factors directly from currently enrolled senior scholars and alumni.

Research Labs & Grants

PhD lab availability, state equipment, publishing freedom, and professor project grants.

Food & Mess Quality

Hygiene, daily menu variety, regional taste availability, and student mess committees.

Hostel Living

Room size, 24/7 electricity, Wi-Fi speed, washroom cleanliness, and curfew rules.

Campus Environment

Green space, library hours, clubs, sports facilities, security, and inclusive culture.

Faculty Approach

Teaching quality, attendance leniency, grading fairness, and mentorship support.
